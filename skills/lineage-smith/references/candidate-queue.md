# candidate-queue.md — the not-yet-assimilated legends, scored

The queue is the corpus's expansion territory: the ranked list of
public figures whose method-bearing corpus is documented, whose
ground the house has NOT yet claimed, and whose assimilation would
grow the stack. **Nothing here is claimed as assimilated** — the
lineage registry is the only record of assimilation, and a queue
entry is a nomination with homework attached, not a distillation.
Scores are the M1 gate's five axes (0–2 each); they are re-scored at
every screening, and entries retire with their reasons kept. The
queue's first three end-to-end executions retired Q1 into the shipped
organ protocol-forensics (v1.0.0, the Dan Kaminsky lineage), Q2 into
the shipped organ interior-cartographer (v1.0.0, the James Forshaw
lineage), and Q5 into the shipped organ harness-smith (v1.0.0, the
Tavis Ormandy lineage) — the retirement records below are the template
every future retirement follows.

## Queue discipline

1. Entries are re-scored whenever one is assimilated, retired, or a
   new figure is nominated; the table carries the last screening's
   scores.
2. Retired entries keep their record — reasons, date, score at
   retirement (a declined candidate with reasons is knowledge).
3. The queue never claims assimilation. The registry does that, and
   only after M6's gates are green.
