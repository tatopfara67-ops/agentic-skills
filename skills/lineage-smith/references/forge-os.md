# forge-os.md — the operator-doctrine runtime: notice, think, behave, script

The forge tier's runtime layer for lineage-smith (the 32nd runtime
module on record, the first for the forge tier): the operator's own
assimilation doctrine — the standing brief behind the three method
waves — re-expressed as rules the agent runs. The A17 universality
guard reserves references/operator-os.md for the person-lineage
organs; the forge tier's runtime ships as its named equivalent, this
module. Everything here executes the organ's own method (the P1–P6
protocol of SKILL.md, the M1–M6 phases of method-upgrade.md); the
queue (candidate-queue.md) and the evidence classes
(psychology-catalog.md) are the records this runtime writes into.

## 1. The thinking framework — decision rules

* **Corpus is the credential.** P1's first axis as a perception rule:
  a nomination is read for YEARS of method-bearing public output,
  never fame or follower counts — the queue's eight retirement
  records, each scored on corpus evidence, are the rule proven on
  record.
* **Read in full or bound on record.** The W2 floor as a standing
  rule: a load-bearing artifact marked skimmed BLOCKS the psych-read
  (M2's ledger gate); where a corpus is too large to read
  completely, the census states the bound and its selection criteria
  — a stated bound is a method, an unstated one is snippet-mining.
* **Documented behavior only.** M3's inner-life ban: motives,
  childhood, personality typing never enter a distillation; the
  psych-read reads behavior, and behavior leaves artifacts. The
  catalog's NOT-admissible column is the enforcement.
* **The three-artifact floor.** A pattern appearing in fewer than
  three independent artifacts stays a hypothesis on the dossier —
  unfalsifiable single observations never become method (the W4
  floor).
* **Born with the method layer.** No organ ships without its
  references/method-upgrade.md in the birth commit (M5, hard rule 3):
  A16 debt is not allowed to accrue again, not even for one release.
* **One lineage, one forge.** A figure lands as one new organ or one
  documented upgrade of an existing organ (hard rule 4);
  double-dipping needs a correction on record.
* **The gates ride with the forge.** Validator, tests, selftest in
  the wiring commit (M6, hard rule 5) — "forge now, validate later"
  is the exact failure mode A16 exists to prevent.
* **Retirement is knowledge.** A declined candidate retires WITH
  its reasons recorded (M1's inverted W1 floor) — a declined
  candidate with reasons is knowledge too, and the queue's retired
  records are the template every future retirement follows.

### The opening ladder — where the doctrine's candidates come from

The rules above govern the forge once a candidate is committed; the
ladder produces the candidates FIRST — the doctrine's own opening
move (find the popular figures, read everything public BEFORE any
verdict) made runnable as questions. Every rung is a
nomination-reading question that writes census rows — no rung
scores, distills, forges, or wires; the M1 gate stays behind the
ladder and consumes its rows.

| # | The question | What it hunts | Grounding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | What did the figure BUILD and WRITE — which primary artifacts exist, in which classes, at which years? | the census rows: tool/code, advisory, disclosed bug, long-form writing, talk with deck or transcript, documented interview — each with year and access status | M2's census protocol (method-upgrade.md §1) — the organ's own operationalization, labeled |
| G2 | Where is the HOW — in which artifacts does the method surface in the figure's own words? | the load-bearing rows: the artifacts the read ledger must mark read-full before any pattern claim | M2's read-ledger rule + P1's method-explicitness axis (SKILL.md §3 P1) |
| G3 | What does the record show when the figure was wrong? | the correction rows: post-mortems, retractions, silence-with-dates — the raw material of the registry's correction field | L4's evidence classes (SKILL.md §4; psychology-catalog Part 1) |
| G4 | Whose ground is already claimed — which existing organ would this duplicate? | the overlap rows: new-organ vs documented-upgrade verdicts, citing what the original organ lacked | M1 step 3 (overlap resolves FIRST — the Q7/Q6 upgrade precedents, candidate-queue.md) |
| G5 | Does any pattern repeat across ≥ 3 independent artifacts? | the yield-preview rows: L1–L5 candidate patterns each with its artifact count; patterns below three stay hypothesis rows | M3's three-artifact floor + the lens set (SKILL.md §4) |
| G6 | What exists only as hearsay — which claims about the figure live in press and coverage classes? | the inadmissible rows: claims with no primary artifact behind them — the OPINION quarantine's candidates | psychology-catalog Part 1's NOT-admissible column + Part 2's quarantine gate |

Ranking rationale: nomination priority = primary-artifact depth ×
explicit-method surface × unclaimed ground, in that order. A
nomination whose reading shows hearsay rows without primary rows
(G6 without G1) retires before reading time is spent; a deep corpus
whose method never surfaces in the figure's own words retires at
the explicitness axis with the census attached; an overlap verdict
converts the nomination to an upgrade candidate instead of retiring
it — the queue's Q7 (trust-graph-hunter) and Q6 (the spine) are the
standing precedents. Rows out; no rung scored, no distillation
drafted.

Generation falsifier: a nomination whose reading yields zero census
rows across all six rungs means the record was fame-class (the W1
failure) or the reading stopped at the highlight reel (the W2
failure) — the primary-artifact classes are re-searched before the
retirement lands, and the retirement cites the empty census itself.
A census full of rows but empty of three-artifact patterns (G5) is
the results-only corpus — the nomination retires with THAT reason on
record, never with "thin corpus". A zero-yield verdict is never
excused without the rows that prove the thinness: the verdict
belongs to the census, not to the mood.

#### The ladder's payload kit — the census rows (customize before use)

Generation writes rows; these are the row classes the rungs name,
every one a read of artifacts already in the public record (no rung
scores, distills, forges, or wires; the gate mechanics live in
M1–M6 and the queue's discipline):

| Read row | The read payload class | Writes which row |
| --- | --- | --- |
| artifact row | class + year + access status per artifact (M2's census table format) | census row (G1) |
| load-bearing row | which artifacts a distillation would lean on; read-full or skimmed-with-reason per the ledger | read-ledger row (G2) |
| correction row | documented post-mortems, retractions, silence-with-dates, with years | registry-correction feed (G3) |
| overlap row | the existing-organ mapping + what the original organ lacked | upgrade-candidate row (G4) |
| pattern row | candidate L1–L5 patterns with their artifact counts (hypothesis until three) | yield-preview row (G5) |
| hearsay row | claims living only in press/coverage classes, with sources named | inadmissible row (G6) |
| OPINION row | unartifacted readings of the figure, labeled and quarantined per the catalog's gate format | quarantine row (never feeds distillation) |

Customization knobs, stated in the plan note: the corpus bound (how
the census bounds an un-readable corpus — the stated selection
criteria M2 licenses), the reading depth (which lens rows the
nomination reading tags — the full L1–L5 set or the yield-relevant
subset), and the queue policy (the screening floor: the 7-hold /
8-nominate defaults, or a stricter fresh-nomination bar while the
queue stands empty).

## 2. The behavioral protocol — situation, move, refusal

* **R1 — a nomination arrives.** (The operator names a figure, or
  the expansion territory opens with the queue empty.)
  Move: the reading ladder, G1 first, census rows to the dossier;
  then the M1 score on the census, never on the name.
  Refusal: none at entry — the ladder itself is the discipline.
* **R2 — a load-bearing artifact is marked skimmed.**
  Move: hold the psych-read; read the artifact in full, or restate
  the bound on the census with its criteria.
  Refusal: distilling from a skimmed load-bearing artifact — the
  W2 floor blocks P3, and the gate is the ledger.
* **R3 — a pattern shows up in only one or two artifacts.**
  Move: file it as a hypothesis row on the dossier with its
  artifact count, and note which artifact class would admit it.
  Refusal: promoting it to a distillation line — unfalsifiable
  single observations never become method.
* **R4 — an unartifacted reading is attractive** (it would make the
  distillation sharper than the record supports).
  Move: write the OPINION row, quarantined, with its next-field
  (what evidence would admit it).
  Refusal: feeding a quarantined line to P4's distillation record —
  a distillation line citing a quarantined opinion fails the forge
  gate (psychology-catalog Part 2).
* **R5 — the nomination's ground is already covered.**
  Move: convert to the upgrade path — cite what the existing organ
  lacked; Q7 and Q6 are the templates.
  Refusal: forging a second organ over claimed ground — one
  lineage, one forge (hard rule 4).
* **R6 — the wiring gate fails** (validator, tests, or selftest red
  at forge time).
  Move: revert the forge; fix the skill, not the gate.
  Refusal: committing an unwired organ — an organ that is not wired
  is a draft, not a skill.

## 3. The scripting arsenal — techniques as runnable craft

All three tools are read-only, operator-side dossier arithmetic.
None of them forges anything; the forge's own gates (validator,
tests, selftest) stay the corpus's instruments.

### T1 — the M1 score-ledger validator

```python
#!/usr/bin/env python3
# T1: validate an M1 score ledger. Reads a JSON file with the five
# axes (corpus volume, method explicitness, public verifiability,
# house non-overlap, psych-read yield), each scored 0-2; prints the
# gate verdict: >= 8 nominatable, >= 7 holds a slot, below retires
# WITH reasons required on record. Out-of-range or missing axis
# values are rejected — the gate is arithmetic, not vibes. Read-only.

import json, sys

AXES = ["corpus_volume", "method_explicitness",
        "public_verifiability", "house_non_overlap",
        "psych_read_yield"]

def check(ledger):
    scores = ledger.get("axes")
    if not isinstance(scores, dict) or set(scores) != set(AXES):
        return "REJECT: axes must be exactly %s" % ", ".join(AXES), None
    for axis, value in scores.items():
        if not isinstance(value, int) or not 0 <= value <= 2:
            return "REJECT: %s = %r (0-2 only)" % (axis, value), None
    total = sum(scores.values())
    if total >= 8:
        verdict = "NOMINATABLE (>= 8)"
    elif total >= 7:
        verdict = "HOLDS A SLOT (>= 7)"
    else:
        if not ledger.get("reasons"):
            return ("REJECT: %d/10 retires — a retirement without "
                    "reasons is void (queue discipline, rule 2)"
                    % total), None
        verdict = "RETIRE WITH REASONS ON RECORD"
    return verdict, total

if __name__ == "__main__":
    ledger = json.load(open(sys.argv[1]))
    verdict, total = check(ledger)
    print("candidate: %s" % ledger.get("candidate", "?"))
    print("score: %s/10" % ("?" if total is None else total))
    print("gate: %s" % verdict)
```

### T2 — the census read-ledger gate

```python
#!/usr/bin/env python3
# T2: enforce the M2 ledger floor. Reads a census JSON file of
# artifact rows; any LOAD-BEARING row not marked read-full BLOCKS
# the psych-read until it is read (or the bound is restated on the
# census). A census with zero primary-artifact rows and no stated
# bound retires the nomination with the census attached — the
# record was fame, not corpus. Read-only.

import json, sys

PRIMARY = {"tool", "code", "advisory", "disclosed_bug"}

def check(rows, bound):
    blocking, primaries = [], 0
    for row in rows:
        if row.get("class", "?") in PRIMARY:
            primaries += 1
        if row.get("load_bearing") and row.get("read") != "full":
            blocking.append((row.get("artifact", "?"), row.get("read")))
    if not primaries and not bound:
        return ("RETIRE: zero primary-artifact rows and no stated "
                "bound — fame, not corpus"), blocking
    return ("OK" if not blocking else
            "BLOCKED: %d load-bearing row(s) not read in full"
            % len(blocking)), blocking

if __name__ == "__main__":
    census = json.load(open(sys.argv[1]))
    verdict, blocking = check(census.get("rows", []), census.get("bound"))
    print("census check: %s" % verdict)
    for artifact, read in blocking or []:
        print("  BLOCKED: %-40s read=%s" % (artifact, read))
    print("ledger rule: load-bearing + skimmed blocks the psych-read")
```

### T3 — the quarantine scanner

```python
#!/usr/bin/env python3
# T3: enforce the OPINION quarantine at distillation time. Reads a
# dossier JSON of quarantined opinions plus a distillation draft
# whose lines carry citations; any line whose citation resolves to
# a quarantined OPINION row is REJECTED — quarantined readings
# never feed the distillation record (psychology-catalog Part 2).
# Read-only.

import json, sys

def check(opinions, draft):
    quarantined = {o.get("id", "?") for o in opinions}
    return [(line.get("line", "?"), line.get("cites", "?"))
            for line in draft if line.get("cites", "?") in quarantined]

if __name__ == "__main__":
    dossier = json.load(open(sys.argv[1]))
    failures = check(dossier.get("opinions", []),
                     dossier.get("distillation", []))
    if failures:
        print("quarantine gate: %d REJECTED line(s)" % len(failures))
        for text, cite in failures:
            print("  REJECT: %-40s cites quarantined %s" % (text, cite))
        print("a distillation line citing a quarantined opinion "
              "fails the forge gate")
    else:
        print("quarantine gate: OK — no distillation line cites a "
              "quarantined opinion")
```

## 4. The rigid run-order — step by step

* **S1 — the nomination reading.** The ladder, G1–G6, census rows
  on the dossier (this file's §1).
* **S2 — the score.** M1 on the census (T1 validates the ledger);
  retire-with-reasons below the floor, the queue's table carries it.
* **S3 — the census bound.** State the read bound where the corpus
  is un-readable; T2 checks the ledger's load-bearing rows.
* **S4 — the psych-read.** L1–L5 over the census, the
  three-artifact floor enforced, OPINION rows quarantined.
* **S5 — the distillation and the critique.** M4's registry format
  plus the critique floor list (the W-list for the new organ's
  method-upgrade module); T3 guards the quarantine at draft time.
* **S6 — the forge and the wiring.** M5's checklist, M6's gates,
  the queue's retirement record — in the same commit, or not at
  all.

## 5. Falsifiers — what would prove this runtime wrong

* A nomination that scores ≥ 8 while a load-bearing census row is
  skimmed → the ledger gate failed upstream; the psych-read is void
  and the score re-runs after the read.
* A distillation that cites zero primary artifacts → press-class
  mythology; the forge does not open.
* An organ whose birth commit lacks method-upgrade.md → A16 debt
  accrued in the birth itself; the wiring gate is broken — revert.
* A retirement without its reasons on record → the queue lost
  knowledge; the retirement is void until the reasons land.
