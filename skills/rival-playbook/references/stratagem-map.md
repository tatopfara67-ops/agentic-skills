# stratagem-map.md — the strategist crosswalk, complete

The full lineage layer of rival-playbook: Sun Tzu's maxims mapped to
corpus moves, the stratagem register with its banned family, and the
attribution notes for the modern documented practice. The crosswalk
is the house's interpretation — it claims no endorsement from the
tradition's authors, and every move it licenses is an evidence move,
not a people move.

## 1. Sun Tzu's maxims → corpus moves

| Maxim (hanzi, romanization, gloss) | The corpus move | Evidence discipline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 知彼知己，百战不殆 (zhī bǐ zhī jǐ — know the rival and yourself, and a hundred battles bring no peril) | the census, R1 | both sides, same axes, every row artifact-cited and date-pinned |
| 不战而屈人之兵 (bù zhàn ér qū rén zhī bīng — win without fighting) | the duel rule, R4 | measured axes beat marketing; the hype war is refused |
| 避实击虚 (bì shí jī xū — avoid strength, strike weakness) | the hollow map, R2 | hollow verdicts cite census rows; no hollow without rows |
| 先胜而后求战 (xiān shèng ér hòu qiú zhàn — win first, then seek battle) | gates before claims | validator, tests, and auditor pass BEFORE any market claim ships — the campaign is won in the wiring commit |
| 兵贵神速 (bīng guì shén sù — in war, speed is precious) | the loop, R5 | census refresh rides the release train; staleness is stamped |
| 以迂为直 (yǐ wū wéi zhí — make the devious route the direct one) | niche encirclement | the unclaimed ground (legend tier, mirror hunting, method layer) is the fastest road to the mainstream, not a detour |
| 兵无常势 (bīng wú cháng shì — there is no constant condition) | the retirement rule | the map dies the day the market moves; corrections on record |

The glosses follow the public-domain Giles translation's sense; the
house read it for the maxims used here, nothing more claimed.

## 2. The stratagem register (三十六计 — selected)

The 36 stratagems are folklore-class: no single author, a tradition
of attributed tricks. The register keeps only the stratagems whose
mechanism is preparation or positioning, and BANS the family whose
mechanism is deceiving people.

### Licensed (preparation and positioning)

| Stratagem (hanzi, gloss) | The corpus move, licensed form |
| --- | --- |
| 反客为主 (fǎn kè wéi zhǔ — turn the guest into the host) | define the evaluation standard: publish the census axes and grade ourselves on them first; the market grades rivals on OUR axes because ours are the ones with artifacts behind them |
| 抛砖引玉 (pāo zhuān yǐn yù — throw a brick to attract jade) | ship one small disciplined module — a validator, a benchmark — and let the ecosystem's response reveal what rivals actually have; the brick is real work, never a decoy |
| 走为上 (zǒu wéi shàng — retirement as the best move) | abandon axes where a rival is strong AND disciplined; contesting owned ground is waste (the pressure principle's corollary) |

### Banned (deception of people)

| Stratagem (hanzi, gloss) | Why banned |
| --- | --- |
| 声东击西 (shēng dōng jī xī — feint east, strike west) | the mechanism is an un-citable claim about intent; hard rule 3 kills it |
| 暗度陈仓 (àn dù chén cāng — the concealed crossing) | concealed movement requires concealment; the census is public and pinned or it is nothing |
| 无中生有 (wú zhōng shēng yǒu — fabrication from nothing) | literally fabrication; the house's founding ban |
| 借刀杀人 (jiè dāo shā rén — the borrowed knife) | turning third parties against a rival — poison twice over, and a stranger's blade is never evidence-disciplined |

The ban is mechanical, not moralistic: a stratagem whose execution
REQUIRES an un-citable claim cannot pass the census protocol, so it
is not a move this corpus can run. Preparation-family stratagems
pass; deception-family stratagems fail the gate by construction.

## 3. The modern documented practice

### 压强原则 (the pressure principle)

Ren Zhengfei's publicized operating doctrine for concentrated R&D:
disproportionate force applied to a narrow point until it breaks
through. Applied here as R3/M3 — at most two hollows, concentration
until the axis is owned by mechanical gates. Attribution note: the
principle is documented in his public speeches and writings; the
corpus's application to skill-corpus competition is the house's
interpretation, on record here and nowhere else.

### 农村包围城市 (encircling the cities from the countryside)

The documented guerrilla strategy of contesting the periphery before
the center: build dominance where the strong are absent, let the
periphery's strength compound into the mainstream contest. Applied
here as R5's niche-first path — the legend tier, mirror hunting, and
the method layer are the countryside; the generic checklist market
is the city, entered last and only with gates in hand. Same
attribution note: documented strategy, house interpretation.

## 4. Reading the map honestly

1. The maxims and stratagems are the tradition's, the moves are the
   house's — the crosswalk claims interpretation, not authorship.
2. Every licensed move resolves to an evidence action (census row,
   gate run, pinned claim); if a move cannot be so resolved, it
   belongs in the banned family regardless of which tradition it
   cites.
3. The map is versioned with the organ: when the census protocol
   changes, this crosswalk changes with it, and the charter's
   attribution discipline rides along.
