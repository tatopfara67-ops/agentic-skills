## Primary sources (public record, consulted)

* **The public historical record of the 1971–72 "blue box" era**:
  John Draper's discovery that a toy whistle packaged in Cap'n
  Crunch cereal emitted a ~2600 Hz tone — the telephone network's
  in-band supervisory signal — and that a precisely-forged tone was
  indistinguishable from a legitimate one. Documented across the
  phreaking-era literature and every credible history of the
  period.
* **Ron Rosenbaum, "Secrets of the Little Blue Box", Esquire
  (October 1971)** — the foundational public text of phone phreaking,
  consulted for the in-band-signaling anatomy (control channel
  riding the data channel) that this organ's doctrine generalizes.

## What this organ does NOT claim

* No telephony exploitation instructions — the in-band signaling
  vulnerability class it references was retired from the public
  switched network decades ago; the organ's content is the
  POSTURE (grammar calibration + minimal forged variation)
  applied to web input validation.
* The input ladder, seam map, and variation catalog are this
  organ's own operationalization, clearly labeled as such.
* No claim of endorsement by John Draper or anyone associated
  with him.
* The operator's brief described the doctrine in the operator's
  own words ("understand the frequency, type, and format of input
  the filter expects; send one or two precisely-modified
  variations — byte or encoding manipulation — to fool validation
  without triggering the alarm"); the public record above was
