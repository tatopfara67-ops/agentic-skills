## 0. The engagement-fit verdict (the Q8 gate, argued and closed)

The queue's stated doubt: "the physical domain sits at the edge of
the house's engagement scope; the forge must argue the fit before
the organ is drafted." The argument, honestly:

1. **What an AI agent cannot do.** Carry a shim, walk a hallway,
   trigger a REX sensor with freeze spray, hold a fake badge under
   questioning. Shipping those as executable runtime would be
   theater, and the house does not ship theater. This is the
   strongest form of the doubt, and it stands.
2. **What the corpus actually contains that an agent CAN
   execute.** The lineage's method is not its hands — it is a
   decision logic about trust boundaries, and the logic is
   information: the cost-ordered entry ladder (enumerate entries
   cheapest-first, record every verdict, report the cheap
   success); the egress reversal (census the opens-from-inside
   surfaces and their trust models — pre-auth, status,
   unsubscribe, webhook, error paths); the OR-composition
   weakness (Matt Blaze's published, algorithmic
   rights-amplification attack — component isolation against an
   OR-oracle, directly executable against any web decision point
   that ORs authorization conditions); the
   installation-over-design audit (configuration and deployment
   error before cipher analysis); the credential-format
   assumption audit (bit-exact structure tables — the Wiegand
   26-bit row and its OSDP AES-128 fix-pair are public standard
   record); the detection-as-finding measurement (Resist/
   Recognize/React); the fix-pair closure.
3. **Where the surface is real in this house's engagements.**
   Bug bounty and authorized scopes increasingly include
   access-control-adjacent devices and services: door controllers
   with network interfaces, badge enrollment portals, IP cameras
   and VMS platforms, elevator and OT controllers, smart locks,
   kiosks. The organ scopes to exactly that inventory (procedure
   step 1; out-of-scope devices are BOUNDED rows, never
   attacked).
4. **What is refused, on record.** No covert-entry execution; no
   human pretexting (hard rule 4 — the lineage's social layer is
   human-operator territory); no actuation against life-safety
   surfaces (hard rule 5).

**Verdict: FIT — scoped to the boundary's information layer.**
The physical-execution craft is bounded lineage knowledge; the
decision logic ships as runtime. The gate is closed with the
organ's scope statement, its refusal set, and the boundary rows
against probe-smith (silicon vs system), auth-boundary-mapper
(identity model vs passage mechanism), scan-economist (probe
budget vs entry economics), opsec-discipline (operator trail vs
target detection), and adversary-tracker (halving vs isolation —
different oracle structures).
