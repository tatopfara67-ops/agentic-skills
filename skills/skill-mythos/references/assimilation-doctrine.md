# assimilation-doctrine.md — studying minds, legitimately

Load when studying an existing lineage, or proposing a new one. The
charter's article A3 is a hard rule, not a vibe: assimilation is how
the bundle grows, and this module is the only sanctioned way.

## 1. The principle

The bundle learns from named, public security researchers — their
documented methodology and mindset, not their property. We read what
they published or what was published about their work, understand the
logic, and re-implement that logic as our own decision rules and
tools. Nothing is lifted: no proprietary code, no leaked material, no
unpublished private work, no trademarks or personality rights invoked.
Every assimilated lineage is attributed in the organ that carries it.

## 2. Admissions criteria (all four, no exceptions)

1. **Public** — the methodology comes from books, papers, talks,
   court records, journalism, or the researcher's own publications.
2. **Documented** — specific decisions and techniques are described,
   not just outcomes ("he caught him" is not a methodology).
3. **Attributable** — the sources are named in the organ's references,
   with enough pointer for a reader to find them.
4. **Translatable** — the logic survives translation into probes,
   registers, gates, and ledger records. Charisma does not compile.

## 3. The process (six steps, in order)

1. **Read complete sources.** Full books and full papers — the
   snippet-mining that article A2 forbids exists precisely so this
   step cannot be faked. Where sources disagree, note the
   disagreement; it usually marks the interesting decision.
2. **Extract the decision logic.** Not the tools of 1995 (they are
   gone) but the choices: what did he refuse to do early, what did he
   measure first, what counted as proof, when did he stop.
3. **Map each decision to a modern equivalent.** A cellular ESN/MIN
   clone audit becomes a stolen-credential usage-fingerprint check;
   RF triangulation becomes multi-vantage confirmation; a telco
   trap-and-trace becomes an instrumented canary. The mapping table
   lives in the organ's own references.
4. **Implement as mechanics, not vibes.** Registers, gates, refusals,
   pre-registration — rules the engine enforces, with the refusal
   records to prove it.
5. **Calibrate on ground truth.** The lab (`lab/`) stages a scenario
   the lineage should solve; the audited example
   (`examples/`) demonstrates it end to end.
6. **Attribute.** The organ's SKILL.md and references name the
   lineage, the sources, and what was distilled — permanently.

## 4. Lines never crossed

* No proprietary or confidential material, ever, regardless of
  availability.
* No fabrication of quotes, incidents, or attributions; where the
  historical record is uncertain, the organ says so.
* No personality cult — the lineage informs the organ; the organ
  serves the charter. Quality over bounty (A7) outranks any hero.
* No uncredited borrowing between organs either: internal lineage
  moves get recorded like everything else.

## 6. Proposing the next lineage

Write the proposal as a ledgered EXPERIMENT record: the figure, the
complete public sources you will read, the decisions you expect to
extract, the modern translation you expect to make, and the
falsifier — what would make the lineage NOT translate. If the
falsifier is empty, the proposal is admiration, not assimilation,
and it dies in review.
